    About

    A local Arusha safari house

    We are based in Arusha. Our crews run game drives, mountain treks and cultural days with licensed local guides. We book camps and lodges, cook Tanzanian food on the road, and keep the itinerary honest — no hidden extras.

    People who run Eco Vibe Safari

    Founder & CEO

    Kassim Ayubu

    Kassim built Eco Vibe Safari in Arusha as a local house for honest park days, mountain treks and island rest. He sets the tone for how guests are met, which camps we book, and how crews look after the land.

    Tour operations

    Iren Vence

    Iren runs the day-to-day of every itinerary: vehicles, park fees, lodge rooms, mountain crews and the timing of game drives. Guests feel a quiet trip because she holds the moving parts.
