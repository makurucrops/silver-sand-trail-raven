Who We Are

About Eco Vibes Safari

Since 2018

Eco Vibes Safari is a passionate local tour company based in Arusha, Tanzania. We specialize in creating unforgettable, responsible safari and trekking experiences across Northern Tanzania.

From the endless plains of the Serengeti to the dramatic crater of Ngorongoro, from the towering peaks of Kilimanjaro and Mount Meru to the hidden gems of Arusha National Park and Tarangire — we take you there with care for nature and respect for local communities.
